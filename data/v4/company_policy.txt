Employee Time-Off Guidelines

The organization provides employees with 30 days of combined annual and personal leave.

Leave requests must normally be submitted at least 14 calendar days before the intended absence.

Requests submitted with less notice may be rejected unless there is an urgent business or personal reason.

Unused leave does not automatically carry forward and may expire at the end of the calendar year.

Department managers review requests, while HR may approve exceptions.

Employees taking more than 5 consecutive working days must obtain additional approval from their department manager.
